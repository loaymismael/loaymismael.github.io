09 — A NOTE TO REMEMBER

A little encouragement
can stay with you.

During my final year of high school, I was working hard to pursue my dream of studying medicine. My teacher, Mr. Ayman Awad, wrote these words to encourage me:

“You are only to write prescriptions.”

I didn't get into medical school, and accepting that was not easy. But along the way, I discovered data analytics, a field I genuinely came to love.

I found a new way to bring technology and medicine together, first through Health Informatics, and then through Artificial Intelligence in Medicine.

Some words stay with us. They remind us that someone once believed in what we could become.

MR. AYMAN AWAD — MY ENGLISH TEACHER · 27 AUGUST 2020
