Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Current website status

This portfolio is an initial static version. The contact form does not submit messages to a server. It opens a prepared email draft in your own email application; nothing is sent by the website itself.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the owner directly by email or WhatsApp, the information you include is handled by those services and the recipient. Do not send confidential patient information or sensitive medical records through this portfolio.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, as part of delivering and securing the service. The site uses a Cloudflare Worker and D1 database for visitor analytics. The public Visitors page is designed to show aggregate totals and country-level counts only, not individual visit records or coordinates.

Analytics and cookies

Custom visitor analytics are enabled. The analytics service records visit events and may store country, city, approximate coordinates, page path, timestamp, and a visitor identifier in its database. The public dashboard is designed to expose aggregate statistics only. Hosting and analytics providers may separately process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure their services. The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage if you change it. Google Fonts may request font assets from Google's servers.

Retention

The retention period for analytics records has not been specified in this policy. Do not assume that records are deleted after a particular period.
