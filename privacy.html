Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Current website status

This portfolio is an initial static version. The contact form does not submit messages to a server. It opens a prepared email draft in your own email application; nothing is sent by the website itself.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the owner directly by email or WhatsApp, the information you include is handled by those services and the recipient. Do not send confidential patient information or sensitive medical records through this portfolio.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, as part of delivering and securing the service. This initial version does not run a custom visitor-location database or display individual visitor locations.

Analytics and cookies

No custom analytics, advertising trackers, or visitor map has been connected in this initial version. The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage if you change it. Google Fonts may request font assets from Google's servers.

Future features

If contact submission, analytics, geolocation, or other third-party services are enabled later, this policy must be updated to explain what is collected, why it is processed, which providers receive it, and applicable retention choices before those features go live.
