SITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Current website status

This portfolio is a static website. Its contact form currently prepares an email draft in the visitor's email application. It does not submit messages to a website server or store contact submissions. The visitor must review and send the email themselves.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the owner by email or WhatsApp, the information you include is handled by those services and the recipient. Do not send confidential patient information or sensitive medical records through this portfolio.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, as part of delivering and securing the service. The site also uses a Cloudflare Worker and D1 database for custom visitor analytics.

Custom visitor analytics

The analytics script creates a random browser identifier and stores it in first-party local storage under the key loay_visitor_id. The identifier is not derived from an IP address and is generally reused by the same browser until its site storage is cleared. A different browser or a new private browsing session may receive a different identifier. Unique visitor counts therefore represent browser identifiers, not verified individual people.

The analytics service may store the browser identifier, country, city, approximate coordinates (rounded to one decimal place when available), page path, and visit timestamp. The system records at most one visit for the same browser identifier within a rolling 30-minute window. The public Visitors page displays aggregate totals and country-level counts, not individual visitor identifiers or coordinates.

The owner can exclude a browser from analytics on that browser by setting loay_analytics_excluded to 1 in the site's local storage. This setting is browser-specific.

Local storage and third-party resources

The site may store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. Google Fonts may request font assets from Google's servers. Those third-party services process requests under their own privacy policies.

Retention

A specific retention period for analytics records has not been configured or stated. Analytics records may remain in the database until the database owner removes them.
